# The Peanut Butter Sandwhich


## Assumptions
Ingredients:
- 2 slices of bread,
- The peanut butter in a jar,
- A butter knife.


## Steps

- step 1
- 1.	Lay out the bread
  2.	Put both slices on a plate.
  3.	Spread the peanut butter
  4.	Use the knife to spread peanut butter evenly over one side of each slice.
  5.	Put the slices together
  6.	Place the peanut-butter sides facing each other.
  7.	Cut the sandwich
  8.	Cut it in half if you like.
  9.	Enjoy!
